How well do you actually
work with AI?

Not how often. Not which tools. How well. AIQ Lite measures four AJIR constructs — the human capabilities that determine whether AI creates value or risk in your work.

◈
This is not a skills test
AI can complete most AI skills assessments better than humans. AIQ Lite is designed to measure what AI cannot replicate: judgment, direction, ethical calibration, and the honesty to measure real impact. Questions are drawn from your actual sector and role.
