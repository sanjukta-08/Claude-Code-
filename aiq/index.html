This is not a skills test. It is a judgment test.
AI can complete most AI skills assessments better than the humans taking them. Knowing which tools exist does not mean you use them well. AIQ Lite is specifically designed to resist AI completion — it measures problem framing, critical evaluation, ethical calibration, and the honesty to measure real impact. Every question is drawn from your actual sector and role context. You will be asked what you actually do — not what you know.
The AJIR framework

Four constructs. All irreducibly human.

AJIR measures the capabilities that sit upstream of any AI tool. None of them can be improved by using AI more. All of them determine whether AI creates value or creates risk in your work.

A
Agency
35% of AIQ score
How deliberately you direct and design your human-AI work. Agency is the capability that determines output quality before you open any tool — and the architecture that determines whether AI creates value at scale.
A1 · Directional Intelligence (20%)
A2 · Collaboration Architecture (15%)
J
Judgment
35% of AIQ score
How critically and responsibly you evaluate AI outputs before acting on them. Judgment is what prevents plausible-sounding AI errors from becoming client-facing mistakes — and what determines whether AI use is ethical.
J1 · Critical Discernment (20%)
J2 · Ethical Calibration (15%)
I
Impact
15% of AIQ score
Whether you can actually demonstrate that AI improved your work — not feel, not assume, but show. The rarest capability in the workforce and the most honest signal of sophisticated AI practice.
I1 · Value Attribution (15%)
R
Resilience
15% of AIQ score
How deliberately you evolve your AI practice as tools change. The AI landscape shifts every quarter. Adaptive Velocity measures quality of practice evolution — not frequency of tool-switching.
R1 · Adaptive Velocity (15%)

Score decay

Your score expires. Intentionally.

A score from 6 months ago is not a score from today. The AI landscape has a meaningful shift roughly every quarter. Score decay normalises reassessment as part of practice — not as a burden but as a signal that your capability is live, not frozen.
