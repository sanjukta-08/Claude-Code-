About the Conference

More than a conference. A global movement.

Harvard World Model United Nations Greece is the premier international relations simulation, designed to inspire and empower the next generation of global leaders. Organized annually by Harvard University students in partnership with a local host team, the conference convenes over 2,000 delegates from more than 110 countries to simulate the United Nations and other multilateral bodies.

Through thought-provoking debate, diplomatic collaboration, and hands-on experience, participants explore real-world global issues — and sharpen their skills in negotiation, public speaking, and leadership.

"Recognized for its academic rigor and vibrant international community, WorldMUN is more than a Model UN — it is a life-changing experience."

Committees

The world's hardest questions, on the docket.

Twenty simulated UN bodies. Real topics. Delegates collaborate, draft resolutions, and confront the kind of dilemmas that shape international policy.

GA · DISEC

Disarmament & International Security Committee

Cobalt Mining in the Congo

Address the human cost of cobalt mining in the DRC, where children and civilians are exploited under inhumane conditions. Delegates examine the international community's role and propose multilateral solutions to regulate supply chains, protect vulnerable communities, and ensure ethical resource governance.

GA · ECOFIN

Economic & Financial Committee

International Monetary Fund Bailouts and their Consequences

Through case studies of Pakistan, Egypt, and Côte d'Ivoire, delegates assess whether IMF policies have fostered sustainable development or entrenched debt cycles, and propose reforms to promote financial stability, equity, and accountability.

GA · SOCHUM

Social, Humanitarian & Cultural Committee

Healthcare Treatment and the Stigmas Surrounding its Provision

Examine how cultural norms, legal restrictions, and economic barriers limit access to sexual and reproductive health and rights worldwide — with focus on marginalized populations in low-resource and conservative settings.

GA · SPECPOL

Special Political & Decolonization Committee

Protecting and Preserving Biodiversity

Address the global biodiversity crisis — wildlife populations have declined nearly 70% since 1970. Delegates debate cooperative strategies for preserving global biodiversity and securing a sustainable future.

GA · LEGAL

Legal Committee

Digital Learning and Educational Inequities

Investigate how disparities in digital access and literacy widen educational inequalities. Develop rights-based frameworks to support equitable digital learning worldwide — covering teacher training, student access, funding, and data privacy.

ECOSOC · CSocD

Commission for Social Development

Collectivized & Collaborative Action for National Development

Drawing on models like the Harlem Children's Zone, delegates consider how systems-level interventions — coordinated across education, health, and economic support — can be adapted globally to drive effective poverty reduction.

About Harvard WorldMUN

The world's most diverse collegiate MUN.

Harvard World Model United Nations is the world's most diverse collegiate Model United Nations conference, bringing together thousands of university students from over 110 countries each year. With a mission to foster authentic cross-cultural connections, WorldMUN travels to a different international city annually.

At its heart is the WorldMUN Spirit — a guiding principle that encourages friendship, cooperation, and deeper understanding across borders. The conference has featured keynote addresses by global leaders, including Pope Francis and King Felipe VI of Spain.

In an ever-evolving global landscape, WorldMUN stands as a symbol of collaboration, cooperation, and hope for the next generation of changemakers.
